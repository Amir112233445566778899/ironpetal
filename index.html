Quick start

Three steps to use Ironpetal safely.

Step 1Create an Ironpetal account if you want comments or public product login. Accounts stay on this device unless you export them.
Step 2Open a product from the home grid. Some products ask: public shared login or your own login inside that product.
Step 3Use the thin Ironpetal bar inside products to return home. Never share your password.
